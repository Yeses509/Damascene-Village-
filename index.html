Good to know

Before you join us.

Questions about your visit? Call us for the quickest help with same-day requests and larger groups.

(708) 994-6984
Is the menu halal?+

Damascus Village serves halal food. For specific ingredient or dietary questions, please speak with the team when ordering.

Can I order takeout?+

Yes. Build a pickup request from the menu, then email or call the restaurant to confirm availability, timing and final total.

Do you welcome large groups?+

Yes. Use the reservation form for parties up to 12; call for larger groups, private events or breakfast trays for four to six guests.

Is parking available?+

Parking conditions can vary. Call the restaurant before your visit if you need current parking guidance or accessible arrival information.
